### IT Service Provider | Cybersecurity Research | Open-Source Tooling

**Pacific Northwest Computers** is a specialized IT services provider based in Vancouver, WA. This organization serves as the central hub for automation toolkits, security research, and infrastructure projects developed throughout 20+ years of solving real-world technical challenges for small businesses.

---

### 🛡️ Featured Projects

We build and maintain tools designed for the "boots on the ground" technician, focusing on automation, deployment, and security auditing.

* **[IT Manual](https://pnw-computers.github.io/IT-Manual/)** – A comprehensive knowledge base for independent IT technicians, MSPs, and repair shops.
* **[Master OSINT Guide](https://github.com/PNW-Computers/osint)** – A Comprehensive OSINT Toolkit for Scam & Fraud Investigation
* **[ESP32 HomeAssistant Alarm](https://github.com/PNW-Computers/ESP32_HA_Alarm)** – A professional-grade, distributed vehicle security system that intergrates with HomeAssistant.

---

### 🧪 Research & Development

Our team actively explores emerging technologies to better serve our clients and the security community:

* **Hardware & Pentesting:** Security assessments utilizing HackRF One, Flipper Zero, and WiFi Pineapple.
* **Resilient Networking:** Development of PNWC_MESH, a Reticulum LoRa mesh network utilizing LilyGO T3S3 hardware.
* **Self-Hosted Infrastructure:** Optimizing stacks centered on TrueNAS SCALE, Proxmox VE, and OPNsense.
