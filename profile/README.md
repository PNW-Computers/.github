### IT Service Provider | Cybersecurity Research | Open-Source Tooling

**Pacific Northwest Computers** is a specialized IT services provider based in Vancouver, WA, serving the SW Washington and Portland metro area. This organization serves as the central hub for the automation toolkits, security research, and infrastructure projects developed throughout 20+ years of solving real-world technical challenges for small businesses.

---

### 🛡️ Featured Open-Source Projects

We build and maintain tools designed for the "boots on the ground" technician, focusing on automation, deployment, and security auditing.

* **[DeployWorkstation](https://github.com/Pnwcomputers/DeployWorkstation)** – Zero-touch Windows 10/11 deployment automation for MSPs.
* **[SystemTester](https://github.com/Pnwcomputers/SystemTester)** – A portable, scriptable Sysinternals-based toolkit for rapid system diagnostics.
* **[ULTIMATE Cybersecurity Master Guide](https://github.com/Pnwcomputers/ULTIMATE-CYBERSECURITY-MASTER-GUIDE)** – A comprehensive knowledge base for environment hardening and defensive best practices.
* **[Pi-SocialMedia-Poster](https://github.com/Pnwcomputers/Pi-SocialMedia-Poster)** – Self-hosted FastAPI cross-poster for automated updates.

---

### 🧪 Research & Development

Our team actively explores emerging technologies to better serve our clients and the security community:

* **Hardware & Pentesting:** Security assessments utilizing HackRF One, Flipper Zero, WiFi Pineapple, and Pwnagotchi.
* **Resilient Networking:** Development of PNWC_MESH, a Reticulum LoRa mesh network utilizing LilyGO T3S3 hardware for off-grid communication.
* **Self-Hosted Infrastructure:** Optimizing stacks centered on TrueNAS SCALE, Proxmox VE, OPNsense, and Tailscale to improve data sovereignty.
